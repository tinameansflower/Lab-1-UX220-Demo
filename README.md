# My favourite written thing | Lab 1 UX220

Hi, my name is Tina Nguyen, a second-year student at Wilfrid Laurier.
I've written various letters to loved ones. These letters contained feelings of appreciation and depth towards those I cherish and find precious. I can't say I have a favorite letter I've written. But I find tons of joy seeing the people I adore and love see my perspective on how I view them and how amazing they are, as we all tend to self-deprecate ourselves. 

These letters take time, and I put my heart into all of them, so I can't say I have a favorite. My friends and family impact me greatly, and the way I express that is through writing. I'm thankful that they enjoy the letters I've written to them throughout the years.
